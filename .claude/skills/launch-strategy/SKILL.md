# Product Launch Strategy

A complete go-to-market launch plan covering pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch phases.

## STEP 1: DEFINE THE LAUNCH

Parse $ARGUMENTS for:
- Product or feature being launched
- Target audience and ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- Launch date or window
- Key metrics for success (signups, MRR, installs, press hits)
- Budget (if applicable)

## STEP 2: PRE-LAUNCH (4-8 weeks before)

**Audience building**:
- Email waitlist with a clear value proposition
- Social media teaser campaign (what problem you're solving, not the solution yet)
- Content marketing: publish 2-3 pieces that establish authority in the problem space

**Partnership and outreach**:
- Identify 5-10 relevant newsletters, podcasters, or influencers to pitch
- Reach out to potential launch partners for cross-promotion
- Brief existing customers who would be excited advocates

**Asset preparation**:
- Landing page with social proof and waitlist CTA
- Product demo video (60-90 seconds)
- Press kit: product screenshots, logo, founder photo, one-paragraph description
- Launch day copy: email templates, social posts, press release draft

## STEP 3: LAUNCH DAY EXECUTION

**Morning sequence (first 2 hours)**:
1. Send announcement email to waitlist
2. Post on Product Hunt (if applicable) — all team upvotes in first 30 min
3. Post to company social channels
4. Direct outreach to top 20 relationships (personal emails, not mass)

**Amplification**:
- Post in relevant communities (Hacker News, Reddit, Slack groups) — be genuine, not spammy
- Launch partners post their cross-promotions
- Monitor and respond to every comment and mention in real time

**Media**:
- Send press release to reporters at target publications
- Have a spokesperson available for rapid interview requests

## STEP 4: POST-LAUNCH (first 2 weeks)

- Daily: respond to all feedback, bugs, and questions publicly
- Day 3: publish "We launched — here's what happened" transparency post
- Day 7: share metrics update (honest, even if mixed)
- Week 2: reach out to people who signed up but didn't activate (personal emails)

## STEP 5: LAUNCH PLAN OUTPUT

Present a timeline:

```
WEEK -4: [Actions]
WEEK -2: [Actions]
WEEK -1: [Final prep]
LAUNCH DAY: [Minute-by-minute schedule]
WEEK +1: [Follow-up actions]
WEEK +2: [Metrics review]
```

## ANTI-PATTERNS

- Launching to everyone at once without a waitlist or sequenced rollout
- No follow-up plan — most launches fade without post-launch nurturing
- Announcements that focus on features, not the user's outcome
- Not engaging in real time on launch day
- Measuring success only by a single metric (signups without activation, for example)
